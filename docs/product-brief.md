# AstraLedger

## Tagline
See where money broke. Prove why. Prevent it next time.

## Track
Razorpay AI Buildathon — Track 04: AI Finance Controller.

## What AstraLedger Does
AstraLedger is a privacy-first settlement intelligence platform for online
merchants. It reconciles orders, payments, settlement reports, bank credits,
refunds, fees, GST deductions, and adjustments.

It visually explains where a money trail broke, creates evidence-backed
exceptions, finds recurring operational patterns, simulates prevention policies
on historical data, requires manager approval, and measures whether approved
policies reduce repeat exceptions.

## The Problem
Finance teams can see unmatched financial records but still waste time
repeatedly investigating:
- Why did this amount not match?
- Is the money genuinely missing or merely delayed?
- What source data proves the conclusion?
- Who owns the issue?
- Has it happened before?
- How can the merchant stop it from repeating?

## Unique Differentiator
Evidence-First Prevention Policy Simulator with Guarded Prevention.

AstraLedger does not merely flag an exception. It:
1. Preserves source evidence.
2. Explains a bounded likely cause.
3. Finds related historical cases.
4. Detects repeated operational patterns.
5. Simulates a prevention policy against historical labeled data.
6. Requires finance-manager approval.
7. Activates a scoped, time-limited, reversible policy.
8. Measures repeat-exception reduction.
9. Never silently hides genuine missing-money risk.

## Main Demo Scenario
WalletFlow settlements normally reach the bank in T+3, but the merchant expects
T+1. This creates repeated false “Missing Bank Credit” alerts.

AstraLedger detects the pattern, proposes an Expected Pending Credit window up
to T+3, simulates historical impact, preserves genuine missing-money escalations,
requires manager approval, supports rollback, and measures recurrence.

## Financial Safety Rule
A wrong automatic financial match is worse than an unresolved exception.

## Data Policy
The main project uses synthetic, labeled data only.
No real customer, bank, card, UPI, merchant, or production payment data is used.
